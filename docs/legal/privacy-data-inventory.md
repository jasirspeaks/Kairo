# Kairo Privacy & Data Inventory

This document is the technical source of truth for all customer, user, and deal data collected, processed, stored, transferred, and deleted across the Kairo platform. It is based strictly on an audit of the current codebase (`apps/web`, `apps/mobile`, `apps/desktop`, `packages/*`, and `supabase/*`).

---

## Confidence Legend

- **[A] Confirmed by code**: Explicitly implemented in active database schema, application code, or Supabase edge functions.
- **[B] Likely/inferred**: Directly derived from configuration or external service architecture (e.g. Supabase Auth defaults, Vercel edge runtime).
- **[C] Planned/not currently implemented**: Mentioned in historical migrations, deprecated schemas, or architectural comments, but not active.
- **[D] Unknown — requires founder decision**: Ambiguous or unconfigured items requiring legal/founder determination.

## Detailed Data Flow Breakdown

### 1. Authentication & Account Data
- **Entry point**: Web and mobile auth screens (`SignIn.tsx`, `SignUp.tsx`, `AuthScreen.tsx`).
- **Storage**: Supabase Auth (`auth.users`) and public schema (`public.profiles`).
- **Processing**: Client creates session via Supabase JS Client; session token passed via `Authorization: Bearer <jwt>` to Supabase and Edge Functions.
- **Third parties**:
  - Supabase (Auth and Database host)
  - Google Identity (optional OAuth sign-in)
  - Cloudflare (Turnstile captcha widget when `VITE_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` configured)
- **Deletion**: Fully automated via `delete-account` edge function.

### 2. Audio & Call Recording Flow
- **Entry point**: Browser MediaRecorder (`useAudioRecorder`), Tauri desktop audio capture (`useMeetingCapture`), or Mobile (`RecordScreen`).
- **Storage**: Audio files uploaded directly to Supabase Storage bucket `recordings` under the path pattern: `{user_id}/{deal_id}/{conversation_id}.{ext}`.
- **Processing**:
  - Edge function `mobile-recording-review` fetches audio bytes from Storage.
  - Audio < 18MB sent inline as base64 to Google Gemini.
  - Audio >= 18MB uploaded to Google Generative Language Files API (`https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/upload/v1beta/files`).
  - Google Gemini transcribes audio into verbatim conversation text.
- **Retention & Deletion**:
  - **48-Hour Retention Job**: `audio-retention-job` runs periodically via `pg_cron`. For any conversation with `status = 'complete'`, `audio_url IS NOT NULL`, and `updated_at < now() - 48 hours`, the audio file is deleted from Supabase Storage, and `audio_deleted_at` is stamped.
  - **Retry exception**: If review fails or is stuck, audio is kept until review succeeds or account is deleted.
  - **Account Deletion**: `delete-account` edge function explicitly iterates and deletes all files in the user's `recordings` folder.

### 3. Deal & Intelligence Processing (LLM / AI)
- **Entry point**: User enters deal data or submits call transcript/audio.
- **Storage**: PostgreSQL tables `deals`, `deal_state`, `conversations`, `stakeholders`, `deal_evidence`, `deal_risks`, `deal_pillar_history`, `deal_state_transitions`, and `ai_inferences`.
- **Processing**:
  - Edge functions `call-review` and `mobile-recording-review` call Google Gemini models (fallback chain: `gemini-3.8-flash` -> `gemini-3.7-flash` -> `gemini-3.6-flash`).
  - Strict system prompts analyze transcripts against 5 qualification pillars (Compelling Event, Economic Buyer, Decision Process, Budget Reality, Champion Strength), determine deal stage progression, assess risks, and extract buyer quotes.
- **Third parties**: Google Cloud / Google Generative AI.

### 4. Google Calendar Integration
- **Entry point**: User initiates OAuth from Settings or Schedule Meeting modal (`google-calendar-connect`).
- **Scopes requested**:
  - `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events`
  - `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly`
- **Storage**: Tokens stored in `public.calendar_connections` (`access_token`, `refresh_token`, `token_expires_at`, `scope`). Synced meetings stored in `public.meetings` (`calendar_event_id`, `title`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `attendees`, `meeting_link`).
- **Processing**:
  - `google-calendar-sync`: Fetches events between `now` and `now + 7 days`.
  - `schedule-meeting`: Creates events on Google Calendar with Google Meet link and attaches Kairo metadata (`kairo_meeting_id`, `kairo_deal_id`).
- **Revocation**:
  - Disconnecting calendar in Settings triggers token revocation call to `https://oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke` and deletes the DB record.
  - Account deletion calls `revokeCalendarConnection` prior to cascading DB deletion.

### 5. Stripe / Payments Integration
- **Entry point**: Settings page or Upgrade Modal triggers `create-checkout-session` or `create-portal-session`.
- **Storage**: `public.subscriptions` stores `stripe_customer_id`, `stripe_subscription_id`, `status`, `current_period_end`.
- **Processing**: Stripe Webhook (`stripe-webhook`) listens to `checkout.session.completed`, `customer.subscription.updated`, and `customer.subscription.deleted`.
- **Third parties**: Stripe, Inc. No raw credit card or financial instrument details ever pass through Kairo servers.

### 6. Client-Side Analytics & Tracking
- **Audit result**: **No external analytics or advertising SDKs exist in the repository.** No Google Analytics, Segment, PostHog, Mixpanel, Datadog, Sentry, or Facebook Pixel are present.
- **Client storage**: Only functional keys stored in `localStorage` (`kairo-sidebar-collapsed`).

## Founder / Legal Decisions Required Before Final Policy Drafting

The following items are technical or business policy questions that **must** be decided by the founder and legal counsel before finalizing the legal Privacy Policy text:

1. **Company Identity & Jurisdiction**: Legal operating entity name, corporate structure, governing jurisdiction, and official contact/privacy email (e.g. `privacy@kairo.com` or `legal@kairo.com`).
2. **Audio File Retention Exceptions**: Currently, completed audio is deleted after 48 hours, but failed recordings are retained until successful retry or account deletion. Does the founder want a hard maximum retention cap (e.g. 14 or 30 days) on failed audio files?
3. **Transcript & Deal Data Retention Period**: Active user deal data and transcripts are currently stored indefinitely until deleted by the user or on account deletion. Should there be an inactivity expiration policy?
4. **AI Data Usage Commitments**: Has Kairo secured commercial enterprise terms with Google Cloud ensuring customer call transcripts and audio are not used to train foundation models? (The privacy policy must accurately state whether customer data trains external models).
5. **Cookie & Tracking Policy**: The app currently uses zero tracking cookies (only functional auth tokens and local layout preferences). Will marketing analytics (PostHog, Google Analytics) be added prior to public launch?
6. **Stripe Data Retention Policy**: Clarify in policy that customer financial and transaction data is retained by Stripe in accordance with statutory accounting and tax regulations even after Kairo account deletion.
